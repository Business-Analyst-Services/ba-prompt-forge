# Contributors

Every prompt in `library/` records its author in `provenance.author`, and that
field travels with the prompt into every converted framework and every generated
skill. This file is the roll-up.

## How to get on this list

Open a pull request with a prompt that scores 17+ on the
[rubric](rubric/care-get-v1.yml). See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Prefer not to be named? Say so in the PR and we will use a handle of your choice,
or leave the author field as `Anonymous`.

## Field reports

The people who report what actually happened when they used a prompt are doing
the harder and more useful work. They are credited in their own reports under
[`field-reports/`](field-reports/), and negative reports count for more than
positive ones.
