Master – Practice Performance & Continuous Improvement

Measure practice performance and maturity, and drive continuous improvement: KPIs that reflect value (not activity), maturity assessment, and improvement initiatives with tracked benefits. Produce on request

Writes: Practice KPIs, Maturity Assessments, Improvement Initiatives For: Practice sponsor (performance story), the practice (improvement ownership)

Sets the assistant up to work with you across this whole area, offering several things it can produce. If you only need one document, use a deliverable prompt instead. Filed under Practice Performance & Continuous Improvement for a Practice Lead. Reference PL-05-M. Quality score 19/20.

1. Have these ready

You will replace each of these in the prompt below:

  • [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]
  • [PRACTICE SIZE, MIX OF PERMANENT/CONTRACT, DELIVERY PORTFOLIO SHAPE]
  • [CURRENT METRICS OR 'NOTHING FORMAL']
  • [WHAT DELIVERY PARTNERS SAY ABOUT THE PRACTICE]

Do not know one? Leave it as it is and say so — the assistant will ask.

Before you paste anything in

My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding.

In short: take out real names, case numbers and anything else identifying, or make up an example instead. See de-identified data.

2. Copy the prompt

# CONTEXT
I am a BA practice lead. I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. Practice context: [PRACTICE SIZE, MIX OF PERMANENT/CONTRACT, DELIVERY PORTFOLIO SHAPE]. What's measured today: [CURRENT METRICS OR 'NOTHING FORMAL']. Perceived performance: [WHAT DELIVERY PARTNERS SAY ABOUT THE PRACTICE]. The audience for this output: Practice sponsor (performance story), the practice (improvement ownership).

# ACTION
Measure practice performance and maturity, and drive continuous improvement: KPIs that reflect value (not activity), maturity assessment, and improvement initiatives with tracked benefits. Produce on request:
1. practice KPI set (KPI, why it matters to delivery partners, formula, source, target [PROPOSED — VALIDATE], gaming risk).
2. maturity assessment (dimension, level with evidence, next-level behaviours).
3. improvement initiative charters (problem, hypothesis, approach, benefit measure, review date).
4. quarterly performance narrative from my raw data.

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Structured, evidence-based business analysis writing aligned to BABOK good practice. Use clear headings, numbered requirements and tables where they aid scanning. Plain English; expand every acronym on first use; no marketing language. KPIs measure outcomes delivery partners feel (artefact quality, rework avoided, cycle time to ready requirements) over activity counts (number of documents); maturity assessed against a stated model with evidence; improvement initiatives chartered with a benefit hypothesis and review date. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example KPI with gaming risk: "KPI: artefact first-pass review rate (% passing peer review without material rework). Gaming risk: reviewers soften to protect the number — counter-balance: pair with downstream defect-attribution measure."

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
Generate: produce the output requested above.
Explain: then break down, step by step, the logic you used to synthesise your answer - how you interpreted each of my inputs and why you structured the output the way you did.
Test: list the source references you used (my pasted inputs, earlier turns, or your general knowledge - label which is which); cross-reference the key facts in your output against those sources and flag anything that does not trace back to a source; and list every additional assumption you made so I can verify or correct it.

3. Paste it into your AI assistant

Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini — any of them. Here is what should happen:

  1. It asks you questions first (up to 5). Answer them — this is what makes the draft about your project rather than a generic one.
  2. It writes the document in the structure the prompt asked for.
  3. It explains how it got there and lists the assumptions it made, so you can check its reasoning and not just its output.

Anything tagged [TBC] it could not verify. Anything tagged [PROPOSED — VALIDATE] it suggested itself. Those tags are your to-do list. Treat the whole thing as a first draft and read every line before it goes to anyone.

# Master – Practice Performance & Continuous Improvement

Derived from `PL-05-M` in the BA Prompt Forge library
(Practice Lead / Practice Performance & Continuous Improvement, scored 19/20
against the CARE + G.E.T. rubric).

## When to use this

The user is doing practice performance & continuous improvement work and needs one of:
- Practice KPIs
- Maturity Assessments
- Improvement Initiatives

The audience for whatever you produce is: **Practice sponsor (performance story), the practice (improvement ownership)**.

## Before you start

The user's work must respect the user's organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything the user pastes is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert them before proceeding.

Collect these inputs from the user. Do not guess them:

1. `[PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]`
2. `[PRACTICE SIZE, MIX OF PERMANENT/CONTRACT, DELIVERY PORTFOLIO SHAPE]`
3. `[CURRENT METRICS OR 'NOTHING FORMAL']`
4. `[WHAT DELIVERY PARTNERS SAY ABOUT THE PRACTICE]`

Then ask up to 5 clarifying questions about anything still
missing, and **wait for the answers** before drafting.

## What to produce

- practice KPI set (KPI, why it matters to delivery partners, formula, source, target [PROPOSED — VALIDATE], gaming risk)
- maturity assessment (dimension, level with evidence, next-level behaviours)
- improvement initiative charters (problem, hypothesis, approach, benefit measure, review date)
- quarterly performance narrative from the user's raw data

Format the output exactly as follows:

> Structured, evidence-based business analysis writing aligned to BABOK good practice. Use clear headings, numbered requirements and tables where they aid scanning. Plain English; expand every acronym on first use; no marketing language. KPIs measure outcomes delivery partners feel (artefact quality, rework avoided, cycle time to ready requirements) over activity counts (number of documents); maturity assessed against a stated model with evidence; improvement initiatives chartered with a benefit hypothesis and review date.

Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience.

## Rules

- Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from the user's inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE].
- Treat the output as a first draft for the user's review, not a finished artefact.
- Do not ask the user to paste real personal or sensitive data.

## Worked example to imitate

Example KPI with gaming risk: "KPI: artefact first-pass review rate (% passing peer review without material rework). Gaming risk: reviewers soften to protect the number — counter-balance: pair with downstream defect-attribution measure."
