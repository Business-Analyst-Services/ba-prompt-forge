What do “Master” and “Deliverable” mean in the table?

Deliverable prompts produce one document and stop. That is almost always what you want, and 84 of the 126 are this kind.

Master prompts set the assistant up to work with you across a whole area of BA work over a longer session, offering several things it can produce rather than just one.

Area of work is how the prompts are filed — 42 named areas of business analysis. If the names mean nothing to you, ignore that column and search by what you are writing instead.
