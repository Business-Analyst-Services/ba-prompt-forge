## Prompt (CARE + G.E.T.)

```text
# CONTEXT
I am a lead business analyst (Lead BA). I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. Engagement context: [PROGRAM/PROJECT, NUMBER OF BAs AND WORKSTREAMS, DELIVERY METHOD]. Reporting period: [PERIOD]. Raw inputs: [PASTE STATUS NOTES, RISKS, DECISIONS NEEDED]. Committee's known concerns: [WHAT THEY ASKED LAST TIME]. The audience for this output: Steering committee members reading in the 10 minutes before the meeting.

# ACTION
Prepare the analysis slice of the steering pack: status they can trust, decisions framed for a yes/no, risks worth executive attention. Draft the input. Flag anything in my raw notes that a committee member would probe, with a suggested holding answer.

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Status: headline sentence + RAG with reasons (an amber must say what turns it green and by when). Decisions sought: each on its own block — decision, recommendation, consequence of deferral. Risks: only those needing executive help or awareness, with the specific ask. Answers to last meeting's questions addressed explicitly. Slide-ready phrasing (short lines, no paragraphs). Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example RAG line: "Requirements: AMBER — billing rules sign-off 2 wks late. Turns GREEN when policy markups land (due 24 Jul, chased 21 Jul). Escalation already lodged: yes, E-04."

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
Generate: produce the output requested above.
Explain: then break down, step by step, the logic you used to synthesise your answer - how you interpreted each of my inputs and why you structured the output the way you did.
Test: list the source references you used (my pasted inputs, earlier turns, or your general knowledge - label which is which); cross-reference the key facts in your output against those sources and flag anything that does not trace back to a source; and list every additional assumption you made so I can verify or correct it.
```
