## Prompt (CARE + G.E.T.)

```text
# CONTEXT
I am a lead business analyst (Lead BA). I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. Engagement context: [PROGRAM/PROJECT, NUMBER OF BAs AND WORKSTREAMS, DELIVERY METHOD]. Artefact types under QA: [REQUIREMENTS / STORIES / PROCESS MAPS / SPECS]. Existing standards: [PASTE QUALITY CHECKLIST OR 'PROPOSE ONE']. The audience for this output: Artefact authors (fixes), Lead BA (assurance view), governance (sign-off evidence).

# ACTION
Review and assure the quality, consistency and completeness of BA artefacts across the engagement: define the quality bar, run reviews with specific findings, and track sign-offs. Produce on request:
1. quality checklist per artefact type (criterion, what good looks like, common failure).
2. artefact review (finding, location, severity, suggested rewrite; summary grade against the checklist).
3. cross-artefact consistency review.
4. review cadence and sign-off tracking structure.
5. themes-to-coach report (recurring issues across the team's artefacts, anonymised).

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Structured, evidence-based business analysis writing aligned to BABOK good practice. Use clear headings, numbered requirements and tables where they aid scanning. Plain English; expand every acronym on first use; no marketing language. Findings are specific and fixable (location, problem, suggested rewrite) — never 'improve clarity'. Reviews grade against the stated checklist, not personal preference; consistency issues across artefacts called out separately from within-artefact issues. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example theme-to-coach: "Theme across 4 artefacts (3 authors): acceptance criteria restate the story instead of defining testable outcomes. Coaching action: 30-min CoP clinic using anonymised before/after examples; checklist item added."

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
Generate: produce the output requested above.
Explain: then break down, step by step, the logic you used to synthesise your answer - how you interpreted each of my inputs and why you structured the output the way you did.
Test: list the source references you used (my pasted inputs, earlier turns, or your general knowledge - label which is which); cross-reference the key facts in your output against those sources and flag anything that does not trace back to a source; and list every additional assumption you made so I can verify or correct it.
```
