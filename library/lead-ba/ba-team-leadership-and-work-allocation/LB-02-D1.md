# ACTION
Allocate packages to BAs with explicit rationale, balanced load and managed risk. Draft the allocation. Where a stretch assignment carries delivery risk, propose the support structure that makes it safe.

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Allocation table: package; allocated BA (role label); rationale (skills fit / load / development stretch); load check (% allocated vs capacity); risk flags (key-person dependency, stretch without support, competing deadlines) with mitigations (pairing, review points). Unallocated remainder and options if demand exceeds supply. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example allocation row: "Package: integration requirements → Senior BA-2 (rationale: interface spec experience, 60% loaded). Risk: single person with this skill — mitigation: mid BA-4 pairs 1 day/wk for succession. Development note: stretch for BA-4, review at sprint 3."
