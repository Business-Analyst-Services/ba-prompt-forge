```text
# CONTEXT
I am a BA practice lead. I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. Practice context: [PRACTICE SIZE, MIX OF PERMANENT/CONTRACT, DELIVERY PORTFOLIO SHAPE]. Data realistically available: [WHAT CAN BE MEASURED WITHOUT HEROICS]. Maturity model preference: [E.G. 5-LEVEL, OR 'PROPOSE']. The audience for this output: Practice leadership; sponsor for the performance conversation.

# ACTION
Define a small honest KPI set and run a maturity self-assessment the practice can repeat yearly. Draft both. Reject any KPI I can't actually source data for — offer the nearest measurable proxy instead.

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
KPIs: maximum 6 — each: what it tells us; formula; source (must be from my 'realistically available' list); baseline [TO BE MEASURED]; target [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]; gaming risk and counter-balance. Maturity: 5-6 dimensions (standards, capability, engagement model, quality assurance, tooling/AI, knowledge), levels 1-5 with behavioural anchors; self-assessment questionnaire; evidence expectations per level. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example maturity anchor: "Quality assurance – Level 3: checklists exist and reviews happen on a defined trigger model with records; Level 4 adds: findings trended and fed into standards/coaching (evidence: last two quarters' theme reports)."

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
Generate: produce the output requested above.
Explain: then break down, step by step, the logic you used to synthesise your answer - how you interpreted each of my inputs and why you structured the output the way you did.
Test: list the source references you used (my pasted inputs, earlier turns, or your general knowledge - label which is which); cross-reference the key facts in your output against those sources and flag anything that does not trace back to a source; and list every additional assumption you made so I can verify or correct it.
```
