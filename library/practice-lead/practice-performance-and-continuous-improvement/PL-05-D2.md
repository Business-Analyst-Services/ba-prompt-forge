# CONTEXT
I am a BA practice lead. I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. Practice context: [PRACTICE SIZE, MIX OF PERMANENT/CONTRACT, DELIVERY PORTFOLIO SHAPE]. Problem to fix: [DESCRIBE WITH EVIDENCE — metric, complaint theme, incident]. Capacity: [WHO CAN WORK ON IT, HOW MUCH TIME]. The audience for this output: Initiative owner and practice leadership.

# ACTION
Charter the improvement initiative tightly enough that in three months we'll know if it worked. Draft the charter. If the problem evidence is anecdote-only, add a 'first, baseline it' step before the change.

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Charter: problem statement with evidence; root cause hypothesis; the change (specific, smallest version that tests the hypothesis); benefit hypothesis (measure, baseline, expected movement, by when); approach and owner; effort budget; review date and kill criteria (what result means stop). Benefits tracking table structure for the review. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example kill criterion: "Kill criteria: if after 2 sprints of the new intake triage the median time-to-ready hasn't moved ≥20% (baseline 9 days), stop and revert — do not extend the trial hoping."

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
