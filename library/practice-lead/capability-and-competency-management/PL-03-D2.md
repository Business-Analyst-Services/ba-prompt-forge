## Prompt (CARE + G.E.T.)

```text
# CONTEXT
I am a BA practice lead. I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. Practice context: [PRACTICE SIZE, MIX OF PERMANENT/CONTRACT, DELIVERY PORTFOLIO SHAPE]. Framework: [PASTE OR REFERENCE]. Assessment inputs: [DE-IDENTIFIED SELF/LEAD ASSESSMENTS, WORK EVIDENCE]. Demand outlook: [SKILLS THE PORTFOLIO WILL NEED]. The audience for this output: Practice leadership and sponsor; anonymised summary shareable with the practice.

# ACTION
Roll up individual assessments into a practice capability picture and plan the development that closes the gaps that matter. Draft heat map narrative and plan. Where assessment inputs look inflated (everyone expert), flag the calibration risk and how to fix it.

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Practice heat map: competency × level distribution (counts, de-identified); strengths and thin spots; demand overlay (where the portfolio needs more than the practice has). Development plan: per priority gap — target state, routes (assignment rotation, mentoring pairs, training, hiring), cost/effort feel, time-to-impact, owner. Sequenced: delivery-critical first. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example demand overlay: "Technical analysis: portfolio needs proficient ×4 from Oct (integration program) — practice has ×2 proficient, ×1 expert. Route: hire ×1 (lead time 10 wks — start now), develop ×1 (BA-7 via pairing, ready ~Dec). Interim risk: Oct-Dec thin — sequence integration workshops accordingly."

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
Generate: produce the output requested above.
Explain: then break down, step by step, the logic you used to synthesise your answer - how you interpreted each of my inputs and why you structured the output the way you did.
Test: list the source references you used (my pasted inputs, earlier turns, or your general knowledge - label which is which); cross-reference the key facts in your output against those sources and flag anything that does not trace back to a source; and list every additional assumption you made so I can verify or correct it.
```
