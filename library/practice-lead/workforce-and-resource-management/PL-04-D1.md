```text
# CONTEXT
I am a BA practice lead. I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. Practice context: [PRACTICE SIZE, MIX OF PERMANENT/CONTRACT, DELIVERY PORTFOLIO SHAPE]. Pipeline: [PASTE INITIATIVES: STAGE, LIKELY BA NEED, TIMING]. Supply: [HEADCOUNT, LEVELS, DEPARTURES, CONTRACT END DATES]. The audience for this output: Practice sponsor and finance; PMO for expectation-setting.

# ACTION
Forecast BA demand against supply and surface the gaps early enough to act. Build the forecast. Highlight the single most dangerous month and what must be decided now to survive it.

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Demand: FTE by level by month, three scenarios (committed only / +likely / +possible), assumptions per initiative marked [ESTIMATE — VALIDATE WITH SPONSOR]. Supply: trajectory including departures and contract cliffs. Gap chart narrative: when gaps open, at what level, under which scenario. Responses per gap: hire (lead time), contract (cost feel), develop (time), or decline/defer work — with the decision date each response needs. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example gap line: "March 27: gap of 2.0 FTE mid-level under the 'likely' scenario (two contract cliffs + Horizon-2 start). Decision needed by 15 Dec: extend contracts (cost feel: medium) or start hiring now (10-wk lead). Deciding in Feb is deciding to be short."

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
Generate: produce the output requested above.
Explain: then break down, step by step, the logic you used to synthesise your answer - how you interpreted each of my inputs and why you structured the output the way you did.
Test: list the source references you used (my pasted inputs, earlier turns, or your general knowledge - label which is which); cross-reference the key facts in your output against those sources and flag anything that does not trace back to a source; and list every additional assumption you made so I can verify or correct it.
```
