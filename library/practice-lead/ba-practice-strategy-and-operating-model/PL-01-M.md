## Prompt (CARE + G.E.T.)

```text
# CONTEXT
I am a BA practice lead. I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. Practice context: [PRACTICE SIZE, MIX OF PERMANENT/CONTRACT, DELIVERY PORTFOLIO SHAPE]. Strategic drivers: [WHAT'S CHANGING — org strategy, demand shifts, AI, funding]. Current state: [HOW THE PRACTICE OPERATES TODAY]. The audience for this output: Practice sponsor and IT/business leadership (endorsement); the practice (direction).

# ACTION
Define the practice vision, operating model and roadmap: what role the BA practice plays in the organisation, how it engages with delivery, what services it offers, and the roadmap to get there. Produce on request:
1. practice vision and strategy on a page (vision, priorities, measures, horizon).
2. operating model options and recommendation (engagement model with delivery, funding model, decision rights, span).
3. service catalogue positioning (which L3 services lead, grow, or hold).
4. practice roadmap (initiatives sequenced over [HORIZON], dependencies, quick wins first).

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Structured, evidence-based business analysis writing aligned to BABOK good practice. Use clear headings, numbered requirements and tables where they aid scanning. Plain English; expand every acronym on first use; no marketing language. Strategy on a page discipline: vision, 3-4 strategic priorities, measures. Operating model options presented with trade-offs (centralised / federated / hybrid) before a recommendation; the service catalogue is the L3 catalogue — build on it, don't reinvent. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example priority (strategy on a page): "Priority 2 — Analysis ready when delivery needs it: move from BAs assigned after funding to discovery analysis in the intake pipeline. Measure: % of initiatives entering delivery with validated problem statement (baseline [TO BE MEASURED] → 80%)."

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
Generate: produce the output requested above.
Explain: then break down, step by step, the logic you used to synthesise your answer - how you interpreted each of my inputs and why you structured the output the way you did.
Test: list the source references you used (my pasted inputs, earlier turns, or your general knowledge - label which is which); cross-reference the key facts in your output against those sources and flag anything that does not trace back to a source; and list every additional assumption you made so I can verify or correct it.
```
