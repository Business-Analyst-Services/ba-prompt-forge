## Prompt (CARE + G.E.T.)

```text
# CONTEXT
I am a BA practice lead. I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. Practice context: [PRACTICE SIZE, MIX OF PERMANENT/CONTRACT, DELIVERY PORTFOLIO SHAPE]. Template needed: [ARTEFACT TYPE — e.g. business needs statement, options paper, interface spec]. Best existing examples: [PASTE ONE OR TWO GOOD ONES IF AVAILABLE]. The audience for this output: BAs completing it; reviewers judging against it.

# ACTION
Design the template: structure, per-section guidance, quality criteria, and a completed example. Draft the template. Mark sections optional-for-small-work explicitly — template bloat is the main adoption killer.

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Template: sections in order, each with — purpose (one line), guidance (what to write, what to avoid), quality criterion (how a reviewer judges it), and example content (from my good examples or realistic synthetic). Front matter: when to use this template, sizing guidance (what to cut for small pieces of work). Review checklist derived from the section criteria. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example per-section guidance: "Section: Consequence of no decision (options paper). Purpose: forces urgency honesty. Guidance: state what happens at +2 and +6 weeks; if the honest answer is 'nothing much', say so — the paper may not need a steering slot. Quality criterion: consequence is specific and time-bound, not 'delays will occur'."

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
Generate: produce the output requested above.
Explain: then break down, step by step, the logic you used to synthesise your answer - how you interpreted each of my inputs and why you structured the output the way you did.
Test: list the source references you used (my pasted inputs, earlier turns, or your general knowledge - label which is which); cross-reference the key facts in your output against those sources and flag anything that does not trace back to a source; and list every additional assumption you made so I can verify or correct it.
```
