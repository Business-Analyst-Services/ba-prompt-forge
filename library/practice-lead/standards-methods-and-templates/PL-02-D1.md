## Prompt (CARE + G.E.T.)

```text
# CONTEXT
I am a BA practice lead. I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. Practice context: [PRACTICE SIZE, MIX OF PERMANENT/CONTRACT, DELIVERY PORTFOLIO SHAPE]. Standard to draft: [TOPIC — e.g. requirements writing, process levelling (L1-L6 hierarchy), story slicing]. Existing material: [PASTE FRAGMENTS/CURRENT PRACTICE]. Failures it must prevent: [LIST REAL EXAMPLES]. The audience for this output: Practice BAs at all levels; Lead BAs reviewing against it.

# ACTION
Draft the playbook chapter or standard so a mid-level BA can apply it unaided. Draft it. Every MUST that lacks a named failure gets demoted to SHOULD — show me which ones you demoted.

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Structure: when this applies (and doesn't); the standard itself (mandatory elements marked MUST with the failure each prevents; recommended marked SHOULD); method steps; one fully worked example using realistic (synthetic) content from our own domain; common failure gallery (bad example → why it fails → corrected version); quality checklist for self-review. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example failure-gallery entry: "Bad: 'Process cases efficiently' (Level? Task? — fails levelling: not verb-noun, not assignable, spans an entire Activity). Fix: Level 5 Task 'Validate supporting document' — verb-noun, one role, decomposes into Level 6 steps."

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
Generate: produce the output requested above.
Explain: then break down, step by step, the logic you used to synthesise your answer - how you interpreted each of my inputs and why you structured the output the way you did.
Test: list the source references you used (my pasted inputs, earlier turns, or your general knowledge - label which is which); cross-reference the key facts in your output against those sources and flag anything that does not trace back to a source; and list every additional assumption you made so I can verify or correct it.
```
