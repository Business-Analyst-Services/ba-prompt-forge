## Prompt (CARE + G.E.T.)

```text
# CONTEXT
I am a business analyst (Business BA). I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. The initiative's stated benefits are: [LIST EXPECTED BENEFITS FROM THE BUSINESS CASE]. Current measurement baseline: [WHAT IS MEASURED TODAY, OR 'UNKNOWN']. The audience for this output: Benefit owners, the project sponsor and the PMO; summaries may be lifted into executive and board reporting.

# ACTION
Act as my analysis partner for benefits realisation: define measurable benefits, design the KPIs and measures that evidence them, and assess whether value is actually being delivered, so the organisation can report outcomes credibly and identify optimisation opportunities. Produce, as I request them:
1. a benefits map linking initiative outputs to outcomes to benefits.
2. a KPI definition table (KPI, definition, formula, baseline, target, data source, frequency, owner).
3. a value realisation assessment comparing actuals to targets with root causes and optimisation recommendations.

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Structured, evidence-based business analysis writing aligned to BABOK good practice. Use clear headings, numbered requirements and tables where they aid scanning. Plain English; expand every acronym on first use; no marketing language. Every benefit must trace to a measurable KPI with a baseline, target, data source and owner. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example KPI row I expect: "KPI: Median case determination time | Definition: calendar days from case lodgement to final decision | Formula: median(decision_date − lodgement_date) | Baseline: 14.2 days (FY25) | Target: 10 days by Jun 2027 | Source: case management system | Frequency: monthly | Owner: Operations Manager".

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
Generate: produce the output requested above.
Explain: then break down, step by step, the logic you used to synthesise your answer - how you interpreted each of my inputs and why you structured the output the way you did.
Test: list the source references you used (my pasted inputs, earlier turns, or your general knowledge - label which is which); cross-reference the key facts in your output against those sources and flag anything that does not trace back to a source; and list every additional assumption you made so I can verify or correct it.
```
