## Prompt (CARE + G.E.T.)

```text
# CONTEXT
I am a business analyst (Business BA). I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. Decision leadership must make: [STATE THE QUESTION]. Options identified so far: [LIST OR 'GENERATE 3-4']. Decision criteria and constraints: [WHAT MATTERS — cost, risk, speed, capability, workforce impact]. Decision forum and date: [FORUM, DATE]. The audience for this output: The decision forum (executives); written so a member who reads only page one can decide.

# ACTION
Produce a decision support paper that lets the forum decide in one sitting: clear question, evidenced options, explicit trade-offs, recommendation with conditions. Draft the paper. Include a 'challenge' paragraph: the strongest argument against your own recommendation.

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
One-page structure: the decision required (one sentence); background (three sentences max); options table (option, what it means, benefit, cost magnitude [FINANCE TO ESTIMATE where unknown], risk, workforce/organisational impact); trade-off narrative; recommendation with conditions and first steps; what happens if no decision is made. Appendix: detailed analysis per option. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example options row: "Option C – Consolidate the two intake teams: Benefit: removes duplicate triage (~[TO BE QUANTIFIED] FTE-hours/wk); Cost magnitude: low-medium [FINANCE TO ESTIMATE]; Risk: medium — enterprise agreement consultation required; Org impact: 2 team structures merge, 1 leadership role affected."

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
Generate: produce the output requested above.
Explain: then break down, step by step, the logic you used to synthesise your answer - how you interpreted each of my inputs and why you structured the output the way you did.
Test: list the source references you used (my pasted inputs, earlier turns, or your general knowledge - label which is which); cross-reference the key facts in your output against those sources and flag anything that does not trace back to a source; and list every additional assumption you made so I can verify or correct it.
```
