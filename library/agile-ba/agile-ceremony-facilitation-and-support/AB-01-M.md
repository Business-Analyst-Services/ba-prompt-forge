## Prompt (CARE + G.E.T.)

```text
# CONTEXT
I am an agile business analyst (Agile BA). I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. Squad context: [SQUAD NAME, PRODUCT/SYSTEM, CADENCE e.g. 2-week sprints, TOOL e.g. Jira]. Definition of Ready / Done: [PASTE OR 'STANDARD']. Ceremony being supported: [REFINEMENT / SPRINT PLANNING / SHOWCASE / RETROSPECTIVE]. The audience for this output: The squad (developers, testers, product owner) and attending stakeholders.

# ACTION
Support and contribute to sprint ceremonies so the squad stays aligned and informed: prepare refinement sessions, support sprint planning and goal setting, and support showcases and retrospectives. Produce on request:
1. refinement session prep (candidate stories with open questions, assumptions and dependency notes; DoR check per story).
2. sprint planning inputs (prioritised backlog summary, capacity check, draft sprint goal options).
3. showcase pack (storyboard, demo scenarios in business language, anticipated questions).
4. retrospective support (theme analysis of my notes, candidate actions with owners, follow-through check on last retro's actions).

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Structured, evidence-based business analysis writing aligned to BABOK good practice. Use clear headings, numbered requirements and tables where they aid scanning. Plain English; expand every acronym on first use; no marketing language. Ceremony materials are working artefacts: short, scannable, timeboxed agendas; questions framed to drive decisions in the session, not afterwards. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example refinement item: "ST-214 'Flag expiring documents': DoR — acceptance criteria MISSING; Open question: does 'expiring' mean 14 or 28 days (blocks test design — ask the product owner); Assumption: worklist badge only, no email; Dependency: document expiry date reliably populated (data quality check DQ-7)."

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
Generate: produce the output requested above.
Explain: then break down, step by step, the logic you used to synthesise your answer - how you interpreted each of my inputs and why you structured the output the way you did.
Test: list the source references you used (my pasted inputs, earlier turns, or your general knowledge - label which is which); cross-reference the key facts in your output against those sources and flag anything that does not trace back to a source; and list every additional assumption you made so I can verify or correct it.
```
