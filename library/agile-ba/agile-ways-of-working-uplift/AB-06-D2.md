## Prompt (CARE + G.E.T.)

```text
# CONTEXT
I am an agile business analyst (Agile BA). I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. Squad context: [SQUAD NAME, PRODUCT/SYSTEM, CADENCE e.g. 2-week sprints, TOOL e.g. Jira]. Definition of Ready / Done: [PASTE OR 'STANDARD']. Improvement inputs: [PASTE RETRO THEMES / PAIN POINTS / METRIC SIGNALS]. Improvement capacity: [E.G. ONE EXPERIMENT PER SPRINT]. The audience for this output: The squad; delivery lead sponsoring the time.

# ACTION
Turn improvement themes into a prioritised backlog of small experiments with clear success signals. Draft the backlog. Reject vague improvements ('communicate better') — restate each as a concrete, trialable change.

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Backlog table: theme; root cause hypothesis; experiment (small, reversible, one sprint where possible); success signal (observable within the trial); effort; priority by impact-to-effort. Running-experiments tracker structure (started, review date, outcome, adopt/adapt/abandon decision). Explicitly limit work-in-progress on improvements to my stated capacity. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example experiment: "Hypothesis: a WIP limit of 2 on 'dev-complete' cuts waiting-for-test time. Trial: 3 sprints. Success signal: median wait <1.5 days (baseline 4.1). Reversible: yes — remove the limit. Review: retro, Sprint 44."

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
Generate: produce the output requested above.
Explain: then break down, step by step, the logic you used to synthesise your answer - how you interpreted each of my inputs and why you structured the output the way you did.
Test: list the source references you used (my pasted inputs, earlier turns, or your general knowledge - label which is which); cross-reference the key facts in your output against those sources and flag anything that does not trace back to a source; and list every additional assumption you made so I can verify or correct it.
```
