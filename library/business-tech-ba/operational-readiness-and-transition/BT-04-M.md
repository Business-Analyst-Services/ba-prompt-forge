## Prompt (CARE + G.E.T.)

```text
# CONTEXT
I am a business analyst (Business/Tech BA). I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. Solution going live: [DESCRIBE]. Go-live date: [DATE]. Receiving teams: [BUSINESS TEAMS, SUPPORT TEAMS, AGENTS]. Change context: [WHAT CHANGES FOR WHOM]. The audience for this output: Business readiness leads, support teams, change managers and the go-live decision forum.

# ACTION
Elaborate the process and technology transition requirements and support change management so the business and support teams are genuinely ready at go-live. Produce on request:
1. transition requirements (process cutover, data cutover dependencies, access, training, support).
2. showcase/walkthrough session designs with scenarios to demonstrate.
3. desk check scripts for business validation.
4. system transition plan structure (phases, entry/exit criteria, rollback triggers).
5. operational readiness checklist with owner and evidence per item.

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Structured, evidence-based business analysis writing aligned to BABOK good practice. Use clear headings, numbered requirements and tables where they aid scanning. Plain English; expand every acronym on first use; no marketing language. Readiness expressed as verifiable criteria ('training completed by X roles', 'support articles published'), not activities. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example readiness criterion: "Item: operations team training | Criterion: ≥95% of case officers have completed module CT-3 and passed the scenario assessment | Owner: Business Readiness Lead | Evidence: learning system completion report | Due: go-live minus 10 business days."

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
Generate: produce the output requested above.
Explain: then break down, step by step, the logic you used to synthesise your answer - how you interpreted each of my inputs and why you structured the output the way you did.
Test: list the source references you used (my pasted inputs, earlier turns, or your general knowledge - label which is which); cross-reference the key facts in your output against those sources and flag anything that does not trace back to a source; and list every additional assumption you made so I can verify or correct it.
```
