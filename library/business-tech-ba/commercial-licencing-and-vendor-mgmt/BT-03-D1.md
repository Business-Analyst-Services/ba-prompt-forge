```text
# CONTEXT
I am a business analyst (Business/Tech BA). I am working on [PROJECT NAME AND ONE-LINE DESCRIPTION]. My work must respect my organisation's privacy, information-security and data-residency obligations. Everything I paste is de-identified or synthetic — if any input appears to contain real personal or sensitive data, stop and alert me before proceeding. Procurement: [RFI OR RFT]. Requirements summary: [PASTE HIGH-LEVEL REQUIREMENTS]. What we need to learn from the market: [LIST]. The audience for this output: Vendors (questions) and the evaluation panel (guidance columns).

# ACTION
Draft the vendor-facing question framework that will elicit comparable, evaluable responses. Draft the framework. Flag any question that invites marketing rather than evidence, and tighten it.

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Questions grouped by section (company and capability, solution fit, delivery approach, support model, security and data residency, pricing and licensing). Each question: the question text; what a strong answer contains; response format demanded (word limit, table, yes/no plus evidence); link to the evaluation criterion it feeds. Mandatory questions on data residency, information-security standard alignment and subcontractor disclosure. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience. Do not invent facts or figures: mark anything you could not verify from my inputs as [TBC] and any value you propose yourself as [PROPOSED — VALIDATE]. Treat the output as a first draft for my review, not a finished artefact.

# EXAMPLE
Example question row: "Q12: Provide three examples from the last 5 years where you delivered [CAPABILITY] for an organisation of comparable scale, including measurable outcomes and referee contacts. | Strong answer: named clients, quantified outcomes, current referees | Format: table, one row per example | Feeds: C2 vendor capability (15%)."

# VALIDATION (GENERATE -> EXPLAIN -> TEST)
Generate: produce the output requested above.
Explain: then break down, step by step, the logic you used to synthesise your answer - how you interpreted each of my inputs and why you structured the output the way you did.
Test: list the source references you used (my pasted inputs, earlier turns, or your general knowledge - label which is which); cross-reference the key facts in your output against those sources and flag anything that does not trace back to a source; and list every additional assumption you made so I can verify or correct it.
```
