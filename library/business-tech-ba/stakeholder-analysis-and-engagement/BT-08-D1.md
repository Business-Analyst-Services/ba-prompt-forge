# ACTION

Before drafting, ask me up to 5 clarifying questions about anything missing, then wait for my answers.

# RESULT / FORMAT
Register: stakeholder; role/area; interest (what they care about, in their words); influence H/M/L with why; current attitude (champion/supportive/neutral/resistant); engagement approach specific to them; cadence and channel; relationship owner. Keep the language professional, plain-English and appropriate for a business audience.
