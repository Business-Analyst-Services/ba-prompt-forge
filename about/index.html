About

Where this library came from, what is in it, who wrote it, and what you may do with it.

Provenance

The library originates from the BA Services AI Prompt Library (CARE + G.E.T.) v1.0 — Portable edition by Business Analyst Services: 126 prompts across 42 level-3 BA services and six roles, each scored by its author against the ten-criterion scorecard.

It is deliberately organisation-neutral — no client names, no sector framing, no jurisdiction-specific legislation, and every worked example rewritten in a generic case-management domain. Two edits make it yours: extend the context block with your own organisation and sector, and replace each worked example with one from your own domain. The example is the field that most shapes output quality.

How to get on this list

Open a pull request with a prompt that scores 17+ on the rubric. See CONTRIBUTING.md.

Prefer not to be named? Say so in the PR and we will use a handle of your choice, or leave the author field as Anonymous.

Field reports

The people who report what actually happened when they used a prompt are doing the harder and more useful work. They are credited in their own reports under field-reports/, and negative reports count for more than positive ones.

Accessibility

This site targets WCAG 2.1 level AA. In practice that means:

  • Every page works with JavaScript disabled — all prompt framework renderings are in the HTML, and script only hides, filters and copies.
  • Colour pairs are contrast-tested at build time in both light and dark themes; the build fails if a pair drops below its threshold.
  • Semantic landmarks, one h1 per page, ordered headings, a skip link, and a visible focus ring on every interactive element.
  • Framework tabs follow the ARIA tabs pattern with full arrow-key, Home and End support. Filter results are announced to screen readers.
  • Tables carry header scope; form controls carry real labels; nothing relies on colour alone — score badges always carry their number.
  • Content reflows to 320 px, respects prefers-reduced-motion, and follows your system light or dark setting unless you override it.

If something here is unusable for you, that is a bug and will be treated as one: open an issue.
